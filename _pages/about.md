Junteng Liu is a first-year Ph.D. candidate in Computer Science at the Hong Kong University of Science and Technology (HKUST) and a member of the HKUST NLP Group. Professor Junxian He is his Ph.D. supervisor. He graduated from Shanghai Jiao Tong University (SJTU) in June 2024 after completing the B.Eng. program from 2020 to 2024. His research focuses on natural language processing and machine learning, including LLM reasoning and reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models (VLMs), and LLM truthfulness and interpretability.

## Skills & Research Areas {#skills}

The recorded information identifies the following research skills, methods, and topic areas:

- **Core research areas:** Natural language processing and machine learning.
- **Specialized interests:** LLM reasoning, reinforcement learning, hallucination in vision-language models, LLM truthfulness, and interpretability.
- **Verifiable reasoning data and logical reasoning:** Synthesizing reasoning data at scale for learning logical reasoning and beyond, as represented by the SynLogic publication.
- **Vision-language model and chart understanding:** Analyzing perception bottlenecks of VLMs for chart understanding, as represented by the Vision4Chart work.
- **Truthfulness and interpretability:** Studying universal truthfulness hyperplanes inside LLMs.
- **Hallucination mitigation:** Using in-context sharpness as alerts from an inner-representation perspective.
- **Foundation-model evaluation:** Building a multi-level, multi-discipline Chinese evaluation suite for foundation models through C-Eval.
- **Parameter-efficient learning:** Composing parameter-efficient modules with arithmetic operations.
